Each broken level is consumed once.

After either directional break, protection resets to the inclusive interval extreme from the broken level's source bar through the breaking bar. This known closed-bar extreme is a protected reference, not a newly claimed fractal. New pivots do not overwrite it; another break must replace it. Historical events are frozen, with their interval bar indices and content ids recorded.
